Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short

  • Star Spot Finder needs no account, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.
  • As the developer, I receive no data about you or how you use the app.
  • What you create in the app (your spots, measurements, settings) stays on your device.
  • For maps, weather and routes the app contacts the services listed below directly. Like any website request, they learn your IP address and the place or map area you ask about.

1. Controller

Thomas Heindl
Ludwig-Thoma-Straße 33, 93051 Regensburg, Germany
Email: wavpixels@gmail.com

2. Data on your device

  • Your spots, measurements and settings are stored only locally on your device and are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Caches: places to stay from OpenStreetMap are kept for up to 7 days, candidates and checked surroundings of the spot finder for up to 30 days, weather forecasts for up to 30 minutes, so they do not have to be downloaded every time.
  • Location: the app only reads your location when you use a feature for it (e.g. the locate button or “My location as start”) and only with your permission (“while using the app”). It is used to show the map there and to compute weather, comparisons or routes for that place. The app keeps no location history.

3. Services the app contacts directly

To work, the app loads maps and information directly from the providers below. Technically necessary are your IP address and the coordinates or map area you ask about – no names, no device identifiers. I receive nothing from these requests.

  • Apple Maps (Apple) – map, imagery, globe and 3D, place search, place names, and car and walking routes. Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Open-Meteo (Switzerland) – weather forecast, seeing and aerosols for the chosen place (at start for the map centre); in the assistant, for times beyond the forecast, the weather of the same days in past years. Terms
  • Overpass API (OpenStreetMap data) – places to stay, spot finder and observing sites: first overpass-api.de (FOSSGIS e.V., Germany), if unavailable overpass.private.coffee or overpass.kumi.systems (Austria).
  • FOSSGIS routing (routing.openstreetmap.de, FOSSGIS e.V., Germany) – bike routes and driving range.
  • NASA GIBS (USA) – nightly satellite images for the “light sources” layer.
  • Amazon Web Services (USA) – elevation data (Terrain Tiles) for the ground view, horizon and spot finder.
  • EOX IT Services (Austria) – satellite image of the ground view.
  • Wikidata (Wikimedia Foundation, USA) – contact details of some mountain huts.
  • GeoJS – approximate location from your IP address, only if you explicitly choose it (when precise location is not available).

With providers in the USA, your IP address may be transferred there. The legal basis for all requests is that you use the respective feature (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR); without the request the app could not show the information.

Links to booking sites, websites of places to stay, Google Maps or Apple Maps open outside the app; the providers’ own terms apply there.

4. App Store

When you download or update the app from the App Store, Apple processes data (e.g. your Apple ID). I have no influence on this; Apple’s privacy policy applies. Crash and usage reports reach me only if you allowed this in your device settings – anonymised via Apple.

5. Your rights

As I do not store personal data about you, there is usually nothing for me to disclose or delete. You still have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection – contact me at the email address above. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority; the competent one is the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA, www.lda.bayern.de).

6. Changes

If the app changes so that other data is processed, I will update this policy. The version published here applies.
